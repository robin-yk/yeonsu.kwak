joule heating

Millisecond pulses that raise methane coupling while suppressing coke.

Non-oxidative methane coupling wants high temperature. So does coke. Holding a catalyst there continuously means paying for both.

Pulsed Joule heating separates them: short, high-amplitude pulses reach the temperature C–H activation needs, then quench before carbon deposition and aromatization finish. Paired with microkinetic modeling, this explained why the pulse train beats steady heating at the same mean temperature, and let methane coupling run in tandem with CO₂ reduction on one bed (Kwak† et al., 2025). The same approach intensifies steam methane reforming and water-gas shift (Railkar et al., 2025).

Moving the heat source inside the tube also removes the fired furnace, the largest CO₂ source in steam cracking. I led the reactor development for an internally Joule-heated, short-contact-time ethane cracker projecting higher ethylene yield at lower capital and CO₂ cost (Mittal† et al., 2025).
