Professional Summary

Catalysis and reaction engineering researcher working on the electrification of chemical manufacturing. I design heterogeneous catalysts and continuous-flow reactors driven by microwave and pulsed Joule heating, resolve their behavior with operando spectroscopy, and carry the resulting chemistry from the bench through pilot-scale operation.
