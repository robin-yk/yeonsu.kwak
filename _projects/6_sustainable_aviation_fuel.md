Bench results do not survive contact with a pilot plant. Two years at the Institute for Advanced Engineering were spent finding out where they break.

I scaled a **continuous 5 ton yr<sup>-1</sup>** process integrating palm-oil deoxygenation, hydrocracking, isomerization, and distillation into jet-fuel-range hydrocarbons meeting **ASTM D7566** {% cite kwak2018hydroupgrading %}. That meant running the plant, not just designing it: rotating shift schedules, continuous operation, and the process-safety procedures that a high-pressure hydrotreating line requires. Two Korean patents on the fuel-manufacturing and hydrocarbon-separation apparatus came out of it.

More recently, at Linde Engineering America, I have been working the same territory on the modeling side — an equilibrium-kinetic model for high-temperature water-gas shift deployed as an interactive web solver, and a UniSim hydrogen-plant flowsheet with design guidance for H<sub>2</sub> recycle, reformer/shift configuration, heat integration, and carbon intensity.

**What I built.** Pilot-scale process integration and operation, the shift-schedule and safety procedures, and the reactor performance models behind both.
